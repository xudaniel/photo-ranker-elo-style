# Demo asset provenance

The optional portrait files under `public/demo/` are separate from the Photo Ranker
source code.

The repository does not currently contain sufficient records to verify their original
source, creator, model consent, or redistribution terms. Until that information is
documented:

- do not reuse or redistribute the demo portraits as standalone assets;
- do not treat the portraits as part of the MIT-licensed source code;
- do not use them for commercial, research, or model-training purposes.

Before a broader public release, replace them with maintainer-owned, purpose-generated,
or appropriately licensed images and add a manifest recording source, creator, license,
consent basis, and integrity hash.

Photo Ranker itself works with images selected locally by each user; user-selected
photos remain in browser memory and are not uploaded to the application server.
